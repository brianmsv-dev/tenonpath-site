# Services and pricing (internal guide, v1)

Public "from" prices are on the site. This file is the internal guide for quoting. Always quote a fixed price in writing before starting.

Kenyan clients: quote in KSh. Websites roughly $300 to $800 equivalent; apps and automations by scope.

## Quoting rules
- Price on value (hours saved, decisions made faster), not on our hours.
- Payment: 50% to start, 50% on launch. Projects over $6,000: 40 / 30 / 30.
- Two rounds of changes included. Extra changes quoted separately.
- Monthly support is offered on every build. It is the recurring base of the business.
- First two or three clients: up to 30% pilot discount in exchange for a testimonial and permission to publish a case study.
- Do not compete with $350 website offers. If price is the only thing that matters to the client, refer them on politely.

## What to ask on every discovery call
1. What systems do you use today, and who owns each login?
2. What report or question takes the most manual time each week?
3. Who will use the tool, and on what devices?
4. Does anything need to write back into a system, or is read-only enough?
5. Deadline or event driving this (investor report, board meeting, raise)?
6. Budget range.
